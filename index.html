Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I shop and place an order?

Shopping with Alibaba Merchant Store is simple. Browse the available product categories or use the search feature to find an item you need. Open a product to view its price, description and other details. Select your preferred quantity and add it to your shopping cart.

When you have finished shopping, open your cart, review your selected products and quantities, then proceed to checkout. Enter your delivery information, choose the available payment method and follow the instructions to complete your order.

2. Which payment methods do you accept?

M-PESA is currently the available payment method at checkout. Select M-PESA, enter the phone number you wish to pay with and submit your order to initiate the payment process.

Visa, Mastercard, PayPal and bank transfer may be displayed as additional payment options, but they are not currently available. Please do not send payments to unofficial numbers or accounts.

3. How do I pay using M-PESA?

Select M-PESA as your payment method during checkout. Enter the M-PESA phone number that should receive the payment request. This can be different from the phone number entered for delivery.

After submitting your order, follow the payment prompt on your phone and enter your M-PESA PIN privately to authorize the payment. Never share your PIN or OTP with anyone, including customer support.

Keep the payment confirmation message for your records. If you experience a delay, check your M-PESA messages and contact support with your order number.

4. How are delivery charges calculated?

Delivery charges are calculated according to the delivery country and the estimated combined weight of the products in your order. Different destinations may have different delivery rates.

To keep delivery costs reasonable, the customer-facing shipping charge is capped at 20% of the product subtotal. The final shipping amount is displayed in your checkout summary before you submit your order.

5. Do you deliver outside USA Market?

The checkout supports customers selecting different delivery countries. Delivery charges depend on the destination and the combined weight of the order.

International delivery arrangements, availability and delivery timelines may vary. If you are unsure whether your destination can be served, contact our support team before placing your order.

6. How can I track my order?

Sign in using the account you used when placing your order. Navigate to the My Orders section to view your order history and the latest status available.

Order statuses may change as your purchase progresses through payment confirmation, preparation and delivery arrangements. If your order status has not changed for an unusually long time, contact support and provide your order number.

7. What should I do if my M-PESA payment fails or is delayed?

First, check whether you received an M-PESA payment request on your phone. Make sure the phone number entered at checkout is correct and that your phone can receive payment prompts.

If no prompt arrives, or the payment appears unsuccessful, do not repeatedly make payments without checking the transaction status. If money was deducted but your order is not confirmed, contact support with your order number and the transaction confirmation details. Never share your M-PESA PIN.

8. Can I cancel or change an order after placing it?

If you need to cancel an order or correct your delivery information, contact customer support as soon as possible.

Please provide your order number and clearly explain the change you need. Requests may depend on the current order status. Once an order has progressed to shipping or dispatch, changes or cancellation may no longer be possible.

9. What happens if I receive a damaged or incorrect product?

If an item arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, contact customer support and provide your order number. Explain the issue clearly and include photographs of the product and its packaging where possible.

Our support team can review the information and advise you on the available resolution. Any replacement, return or refund arrangement will depend on the circumstances and the applicable store policy.

10. How do I create an account or update my delivery details?

You can create an account through the Sign Up option on the store. Use a valid email address and the required registration details. Sign in whenever you want to access your account.

To update your saved delivery information, open your account dashboard and select Delivery Details or My Profile, where available. Make sure your name, phone number, country, town and delivery address are accurate before placing an order.

If you have trouble signing in or updating your information, contact customer support for assistance.
